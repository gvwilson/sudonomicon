The Sudonomicon

The Sudonomicon is an introduction to Unix systems programming and networking for the cautious and weary. It is written for data scientists who have been using Python for years but have little formal computer-science training, and want to understand the ports, processes, permissions, and files-that-are-not-files beneath the shell.

All of the material is available under an open license, and contributions through our repository are welcome. All contributors are required to respect our Code of Conduct.

Greg Wilson is a programmer, author, and educator based in Toronto. He was the co-founder and first Executive Director of Software Carpentry and received ACM SIGSOFT’s Influential Educator Award in 2020.
